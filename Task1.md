# Task 1: Choose a Weather Variable

**Variable:** `10m_u_component_of_wind`

## What it represents

The `10m_u_component_of_wind` represents the east-west directional speed of the air at a height of 10 meters above the ground. Often called the zonal wind, this directional component represents wind blowing horizontally across the globe. 

This measurement increases significantly with altitude. Hence, a measurement taken at this short height is representative of the wind speed experienced on the ground, given that many obstacles (trees, suburban houses, other ground-level objects causing interference) do not exceed 10 m.

## Single-level or pressure-level?

**Single-level variable** — measured at a fixed height of 10 m above the ground, instead of at atmospheric pressure levels.

## Common abbreviation(s)

- **u10** — common short name (as used above)
